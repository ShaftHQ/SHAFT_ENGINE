# Investigate lessons

General rules taken from investigation items. No project names, identifiers,
or secrets. Closing an investigation still follows the
[delivery cycle](delivery-cycle.md).

## Canonical record

A branch, audit note, or planned slot is not the canonical record. Read the
live record before repeating a correction. If the correction already landed,
record the supersession and stop.

## Past approval

A past approval does not authorize a new publish, send, delete, unlist,
purchase, upload, uninstall, or support message. The historical draft stays
unsent.

## Unknown stays unknown

Do not invent an identifier, a URL, or a success. Do not copy a token or
credential into the record.

## Restoration

A later restore does not prove the data never vanished. Name what is lost,
restored, and still recoverable. Prevention is an enforced gate, not a story.
Do not rewrite history to make the record look clean.

## Non-success is not green

Abandoned, cancelled, or missing checks are not success. The SHA under test
is the SHA the rule names. An empty result and an API error are different,
and both fail closed.

## Completed phase

An optional unanswered question does not fail a completed phase. A later
phase starts with its own decision and its own tests. Do not reopen a merged
phase unless that phase is named again.

## Historical inventory

An old inventory is not live work. Verify the current disposition before
reviving a ticket. Do not sweep other repositories from that list. Keep done
and won't-do dispositions.

## Identity acceptance

The human owner accepts a likeness or voice. A model does not accept a
synthetic stand-in as that person. No new recording, purchase, or upload
without a current approval.

## Leaving Investigate

An investigation leaves that column as exactly one of:

- Done: every acceptance criterion has evidence, or a verified supersession.
- Won't do: the remaining action is forbidden or explicitly dropped.
- Waiting on others: a third party owns the next action.

The tracker status matches that choice. A comment states the evidence and
which rule above applies. Do not mark Done for an unverified external effect.
