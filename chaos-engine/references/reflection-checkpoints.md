# Reflection

Reflection is optional evidence, never a tool, mutation, delivery, or completion gate.

Use it when repeated failures in the same area require a changed experiment, or at the actual end of
a long session when a concise retrospective will improve future work. One-off failures, elapsed
time, missing fields, store failures, unavailable GitHub, and absent issue URLs never trigger a
refusal.

A useful reflection states:

- the failed assumption or repeated fingerprint;
- the alternative explanations considered;
- the next discriminating experiment or changed approach;
- observed proof and remaining risk;
- a durable destination only when an actionable lesson exists.

Receipts may be appended with `scripts/agents/reflection.py` when useful. Keep them bounded,
sanitized, and free of prompts, transcripts, secrets, source excerpts, raw errors, and user-specific
absolute paths. Continue and complete whether or not a receipt is written.
