# Video editing: 20 references

Craft card: [../craft/video-editing.md](../craft/video-editing.md). Each line: link, why it is great,
transferable techniques. Ideas are re-expressed; no media is copied.

1. [Walter Murch, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Blink_of_an_Eye_(Murch_book)): rule of six for cuts. Techniques: emotion first, then story, then rhythm.
2. [Every Frame a Painting](https://www.youtube.com/@everyframeapainting): video essays on editing and staging. Techniques: cut on motion; visual comedy timing.
3. [ProVideo Coalition](https://www.provideocoalition.com/): post-production craft articles incl. Art of the Cut. Techniques: how editors find rhythm.
4. [American Cinema Editors](https://americancinemaeditors.org/): editors' guild and Eddie awards. Techniques: award-winning cut references.
5. [Frame.io Insider](https://blog.frame.io/): post-production workflow craft. Techniques: review loops and versioning.
6. [Premiere Gal](https://www.youtube.com/@PremiereGal): practical editing tutorials. Techniques: J/L cuts, speed ramps.
7. [Film Riot](https://www.youtube.com/@filmriot): DIY technique. Techniques: match cuts and practical effects.
8. [StudioBinder blog](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/): film-language glossary. Techniques: cut types with examples.
9. [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/): filmmaking craft coverage. Techniques: editor interviews.
10. [DaVinci Resolve training](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/training): free official editing curriculum. Techniques: edit page, trim modes.
11. [ffmpeg documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html): the open editing engine. Techniques: trim, concat, xfade, loudnorm.
12. [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org/): open-source NLE. Techniques: proxy editing.
13. [Shotcut](https://shotcut.org/): open-source NLE. Techniques: keyframed filters.
14. [Thomas Flight](https://www.youtube.com/@ThomasFlight): essays on film craft. Techniques: pacing analysis.
15. [Lessons from the Screenplay](https://www.youtube.com/@LessonsfromtheScreenplay): structure behind scenes. Techniques: setup and payoff.
16. [Vimeo Staff Picks](https://vimeo.com/channels/staffpicks): curated short-form craft. Techniques: short films cut tight.
17. [Kirby Ferguson, Everything is a Remix](https://www.everythingisaremix.info/): essay editing with archival rhythm. Techniques: argument-driven cutting.
18. [Johnny Harris](https://www.youtube.com/@johnnyharris): explainer editing with maps and paper. Techniques: physical textures in explainers.
19. [Vox](https://www.youtube.com/@Vox): explainer house style. Techniques: annotated footage, kinetic type.
20. [Avid Media Composer learning](https://www.avid.com/learn-and-support): pro NLE training. Techniques: trim-mode editing.
