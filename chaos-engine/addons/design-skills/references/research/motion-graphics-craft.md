# Motion graphics: 20 references

Craft card: [../craft/motion-graphics-craft.md](../craft/motion-graphics-craft.md). Each line: link, why it is great,
transferable techniques. Ideas are re-expressed; no media is copied.

1. [motionmaxxing](https://github.com/Tejashmakwana/motionmaxxing): measured motion grammar from 53 pro moments (Apache-2.0). Techniques: land decelerates, leave accelerates; one spring per film.
2. [Motionographer](https://motionographer.com/): the motion design publication. Techniques: study featured pieces' structure.
3. [Buck](https://buck.co/): top motion studio. Techniques: craft in character and type.
4. [ManvsMachine](https://mvsm.com/): 3D and type motion studio. Techniques: material-driven abstraction.
5. [Ordinary Folk](https://ordinaryfolk.co/): rigour in 2D motion. Techniques: clean overlap and follow-through.
6. [Gunner](https://gunner.work/): playful 2D/3D studio. Techniques: character timing.
7. [School of Motion](https://www.schoolofmotion.com/): motion design education. Techniques: graph-editor easing.
8. [Ben Marriott](https://www.youtube.com/@BenMarriott): After Effects craft. Techniques: shape-layer animation.
9. [Jake in Motion](https://www.youtube.com/@JakeInMotion): After Effects techniques. Techniques: expressions and rigs.
10. [The 12 principles (Disney)](https://www.disneyanimation.com/): animation fundamentals. Techniques: anticipation, overlap, slow in/out.
11. [GSAP docs and ease visualiser](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Eases/): reference eases. Techniques: power, expo, custom ease.
12. [Material motion](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview): documented motion tokens. Techniques: emphasized decelerate 400-500 ms.
13. [Apple HIG motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion): purposeful motion guidance. Techniques: respect reduce motion.
14. [Lottie](https://airbnb.io/lottie/): vector animation runtime. Techniques: export once, play everywhere.
15. [Rive](https://rive.app/): interactive motion with state machines. Techniques: stateful UI animation.
16. [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/): video as React code. Techniques: frame-pure compositions.
17. [Motion Canvas](https://motioncanvas.io/): code-driven animation. Techniques: generators for timing.
18. [Theatre.js](https://www.theatrejs.com/): keyframe editor for web. Techniques: tweak curves visually.
19. [Art of the Title](https://www.artofthetitle.com/): title sequence archive. Techniques: title sequences as idea.
20. [Manim](https://www.manim.community/): math animation engine. Techniques: transform-based explanation.
