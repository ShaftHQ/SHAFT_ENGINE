# Logo design and identity: 20 references

Craft card: [../craft/logo-identity.md](../craft/logo-identity.md). Each line: link, why it is great,
transferable techniques. Ideas are re-expressed; no media is copied.

1. [Brand New (UnderConsideration)](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/): the standing critique of identity redesigns. Techniques: before/after reading; polls show public reaction.
2. [Logobook](https://www.logobook.com/): archive of historic marks. Techniques: reduce to geometry.
3. [Chermayeff & Geismar & Haviv](https://www.cghnyc.com/): identities that last decades. Techniques: simple, distinctive, appropriate test.
4. [Wolff Olins](https://www.wolffolins.com/): identity systems over static logos. Techniques: flexible marks; behaviour as identity.
5. [Koto](https://koto.studio/): contemporary tech identities. Techniques: motion-ready marks.
6. [DesignStudio](https://design.studio/): large brand system rebuilds. Techniques: brand behaviours and toolkits.
7. [Collins](https://www.wearecollins.com/): strategic identity studio. Techniques: idea-led brand platforms.
8. [Standards Manual (Order)](https://standardsmanual.com/): reissued standards manuals. Techniques: rules-led identity documents.
9. [NASA Graphics Standards Manual](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/nasa_graphics_manual_nhb_1430-2_jan_1976.pdf): 1975 worm manual. Techniques: usage, clear space, misuse pages.
10. [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/): official SVG brand marks. Techniques: fetch official marks, never redraw.
11. [SVGL](https://svgl.app/): library of brand SVG logos. Techniques: light/dark variants.
12. [Michael Bierut, How to](https://www.pentagram.com/about/michael-bierut): process essays from a Pentagram partner. Techniques: solve the problem, then style.
13. [Aaron Draplin](https://www.draplin.com/): thick-line utilitarian marks. Techniques: sketch volume before vectors.
14. [Logo Design Love](https://www.logodesignlove.com/): David Airey's identity practice. Techniques: briefs, sketches, presentations.
15. [Identity Designed](https://identitydesigned.com/): identity case studies. Techniques: study full systems, not marks alone.
16. [Mucho](https://wearemucho.com/): bilingual identity studio. Techniques: type-driven marks.
17. [Monotype brand typography](https://www.monotype.com/): bespoke brand typefaces. Techniques: custom type as identity.
18. [Koto on brand motion](https://koto.studio/work/): motion principles in identity. Techniques: mark built from its animation.
19. [Paul Rand (official site)](https://www.paulrand.design/): IBM, ABC, UPS marks. Techniques: wit and reduction.
20. [Saul Bass](https://www.artofthetitle.com/designer/saul-bass/): marks and titles from one hand. Techniques: logo as moving idea.
