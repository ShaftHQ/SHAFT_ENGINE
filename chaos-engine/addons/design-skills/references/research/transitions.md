# Transitions: 20 references

Craft card: [../craft/transitions.md](../craft/transitions.md). Each line: link, why it is great,
transferable techniques. Ideas are re-expressed; no media is copied.

1. [Every Frame a Painting, match cuts](https://www.youtube.com/@everyframeapainting): craft of joining shots. Techniques: match on shape and motion.
2. [StudioBinder, types of cuts](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/types-of-editing-transitions-in-film/): glossary of transitions. Techniques: match, smash, J/L, whip.
3. [Blackmagic Design forum (Resolve)](https://forum.blackmagicdesign.com/): NLE transition practice. Techniques: handle frames before transitions.
4. [ffmpeg xfade](https://trac.ffmpeg.org/wiki/Xfade): open transition library. Techniques: 45 xfade types; offset math.
5. [GL Transitions](https://gl-transitions.com/): open GLSL transitions. Techniques: shader wipes.
6. [Zach King](https://www.youtube.com/@ZachKing): invisible in-camera transitions. Techniques: hide the cut in motion.
7. [Daniel Schiffer](https://www.youtube.com/@danielschiffer): product-video transitions. Techniques: in-camera transitions.
8. [Peter McKinnon](https://www.youtube.com/@PeterMcKinnon): creator-style transitions. Techniques: object-wipe transitions.
9. [Sam Kolder](https://www.instagram.com/samkolder/): travel-edit transitions. Techniques: speed-ramp + whip.
10. [Art of the Title (rhythmic title cutting)](https://www.artofthetitle.com/): rhythmic match cutting. Techniques: sound-led cuts.
11. [View transitions API](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/view-transitions): web shared-element transitions. Techniques: morph the same element across states.
12. [Material shared axis / container transform](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/transitions/transition-patterns): documented transition patterns. Techniques: container transform for continuity.
13. [Apple HIG navigation transitions](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion): continuity guidance. Techniques: spatial consistency.
14. [Framer Motion layout animations](https://motion.dev/docs/react-layout-animations): shared layout transitions. Techniques: layoutId morphs.
15. [Rive state transitions](https://rive.app/): interpolated states. Techniques: blend states not crossfade.
16. [Kurzgesagt](https://www.youtube.com/@kurzgesagt): object-carry transitions in explainers. Techniques: camera travels through one world.
17. [Motionographer features](https://motionographer.com/): award pieces with seamless joins. Techniques: study seams frame by frame.
18. [Animoto (anti-reference: template transitions)](https://animoto.com/): template transitions to avoid. Techniques: spot the crossfade default.
19. [PremiumBeat blog (transition sound design)](https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/): transition sound design. Techniques: whoosh peaks on the cut.
20. [The Cutting Edge (documentary)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0408306/): history of the cut. Techniques: invisible editing.
