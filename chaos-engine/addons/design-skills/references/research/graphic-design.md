# Graphic design: 20 references

Craft card: [../craft/graphic-design.md](../craft/graphic-design.md). Each line: link, why it is great,
transferable techniques. Ideas are re-expressed; no media is copied.

1. [Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/): partner-led studio with a deep public archive. Techniques: idea-first systems; type as image.
2. [Sagmeister & Walsh](https://sagmeisterwalsh.com/): playful conceptual identities. Techniques: handmade elements against digital systems.
3. [Wim Crouwel at Design Museum](https://designmuseum.org/): modernist grid systems in museum archive. Techniques: grid-generated letterforms.
4. [Spin](https://www.spin.co.uk/): typographic studio. Techniques: bold type, few colours, clear hierarchy.
5. [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/): daily coverage of contemporary graphic work. Techniques: track emerging styles.
6. [Eye on Design (AIGA)](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/): critical design journalism. Techniques: read the reasoning, not only the result.
7. [Letterform Archive](https://letterformarchive.org/): archive of lettering and graphic design. Techniques: primary sources for type ideas.
8. [ADC Awards](https://www.oneclub.org/awards/adcawards/): Art Directors Club annual awards. Techniques: judge craft categories.
9. [D&AD Awards](https://www.dandad.org/awards/): pencils awarded for craft. Techniques: study Yellow/Black Pencil reasoning.
10. [Type Directors Club](https://www.tdc.org/): typographic excellence awards. Techniques: annual winners as type benchmarks.
11. [Ellen Lupton (Thinking with Type author)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Lupton): typography educator and primer author. Techniques: letter, text, grid.
12. [Josef Müller-Brockmann, Grid Systems](https://www.niggli.ch/en/produkt/grid-systems-in-graphic-design/): classic grid reference. Techniques: modular grids with baseline.
13. [Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/): indexed archive of real typography. Techniques: see a face in context before choosing it.
14. [Google Fonts Knowledge](https://fonts.google.com/knowledge): free curriculum on type. Techniques: pairing, hierarchy, variable fonts.
15. [Bauhaus Kooperation](https://www.bauhauskooperation.com/): primary Bauhaus archive. Techniques: primary shapes; function-led form.
16. [Swiss Style, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Typographic_Style): history of the International style. Techniques: asymmetric layout, objective photo.
17. [Standards Manual archive](https://standardsmanual.com/collections/all): reissued identity manuals. Techniques: rules as design.
18. [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/): shot-level visual craft. Techniques: use for detail craft, not concept.
19. [Brand New Conference talks](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnewconference/): designer talks on process. Techniques: process over polish.
20. [Paula Scher at Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/about/paula-scher): type-as-image identities. Techniques: scale and density as voice.
