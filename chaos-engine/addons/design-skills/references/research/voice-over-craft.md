# Voice-over: 20 references

Craft card: [../craft/voice-over-craft.md](../craft/voice-over-craft.md). Each line: link, why it is great,
transferable techniques. Ideas are re-expressed; no media is copied.

1. [Gravy for the Brain](https://www.gravyforthebrain.com/): voice-actor training. Techniques: read for intent, not words.
2. [Voice123 blog](https://voice123.com/blog/): casting and direction notes. Techniques: script direction vocabulary.
3. [Edge Studio](https://www.edgestudio.com/): voice-over training. Techniques: conversational read; cold reads.
4. [Voice Over Xtra](https://www.voiceoverxtra.com/): voice-over industry articles. Techniques: read styles and direction.
5. [Booth Junkie](https://www.youtube.com/@BoothJunkie): home voice-over studio craft. Techniques: treat the room before the mic.
6. [ElevenLabs documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs): TTS direction controls. Techniques: stability, style, SSML breaks.
7. [W3C SSML 1.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/speech-synthesis11/): speech markup standard. Techniques: break, prosody, say-as.
8. [Microsoft Speech SSML](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/ai-services/speech-service/speech-synthesis-markup): SSML prosody reference. Techniques: rate, pitch, emphasis.
9. [Google Cloud TTS SSML](https://cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/docs/ssml): SSML reference. Techniques: say-as for acronyms.
10. [Coqui TTS](https://github.com/coqui-ai/TTS): open TTS toolkit. Techniques: local voices.
11. [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper): fast local neural TTS. Techniques: offline drafts.
12. [OpenAI Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper): ASR for checking reads. Techniques: WER against script.
13. [EBU R 128 (PDF)](https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r128.pdf): loudness standard. Techniques: -23 LUFS broadcast; LRA.
14. [Apple podcast audio spec](https://podcasters.apple.com/support/893-audio-requirements): spoken-word loudness target. Techniques: -16 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
15. [Descript](https://www.descript.com/): text-based audio editing. Techniques: edit by transcript.
16. [iZotope RX learn](https://www.izotope.com/en/learn.html): dialogue repair craft. Techniques: de-click, de-noise, de-reverb.
17. [Audacity manual](https://manual.audacityteam.org/): open audio editor. Techniques: noise profile reduction.
18. [Ann Handley, Everybody Writes](https://annhandley.com/everybodywrites/): writing that sounds human. Techniques: cut half; write for the ear.
19. [Plain Language guidelines](https://www.plainlanguage.gov/guidelines/): writing for clarity. Techniques: short words, active voice.
20. [BBC News style guide](https://www.bbc.co.uk/newsstyleguide): writing for the ear. Techniques: plain words, one idea per sentence.
