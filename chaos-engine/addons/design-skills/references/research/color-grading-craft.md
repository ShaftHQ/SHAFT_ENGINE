# Colour grading: 20 references

Craft card: [../craft/color-grading-craft.md](../craft/color-grading-craft.md). Each line: link, why it is great,
transferable techniques. Ideas are re-expressed; no media is copied.

1. [Cinematography Database](https://www.youtube.com/@CinematographyDatabase): light and colour breakdowns. Techniques: motivated light sets the grade.
2. [Cullen Kelly](https://www.youtube.com/@CullenKelly): colour-managed grading. Techniques: grade in a wide scene space, output transform last.
3. [Darren Mostyn](https://www.youtube.com/@DarrenMostyn): Resolve colour tutorials. Techniques: qualifiers and windows.
4. [Lowepost](https://lowepost.com/): colourist courses and forum. Techniques: look development.
5. [ACES (Academy Color Encoding System)](https://acescentral.com/): industry colour-management standard. Techniques: IDT, ACEScct, RRT/ODT.
6. [ITU-R BT.709](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BT.709): HD colour standard. Techniques: primaries and transfer for web video.
7. [ITU-R BT.2100](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BT.2100): HDR standard. Techniques: PQ/HLG targets.
8. [Colour Science for Python](https://www.colour-science.org/): open colour-science library. Techniques: convert and measure colour.
9. [Steve Yedlin, Display Prep Demo](https://www.yedlin.net/DisplayPrepDemo/): essay on emulation and look. Techniques: the look lives in the transform.
10. [DaVinci Resolve colour training](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/training): free colourist guide. Techniques: scopes first.
11. [Company 3](https://www.company3.com/): leading grading house. Techniques: study featured looks.
12. [Juan Melara](https://juanmelara.com.au/): film-emulation and look breakdowns. Techniques: split-tone in log space.
13. [FilmLight Baselight](https://www.filmlight.ltd.uk/): grading system and colour science. Techniques: Truelight colour management.
14. [Frame.io Insider colour articles](https://blog.frame.io/category/color/): colour workflow articles. Techniques: grade with references.
15. [Colorist Society International](https://coloristsociety.com/): colourist guild. Techniques: peer standards.
16. [Netflix partner help, colour](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/): delivery colour specs. Techniques: reference display and tags.
17. [Color-hex palette lookup](https://www.color-hex.com/): palette lookups. Techniques: name and measure palettes.
18. [FilmGrab](https://film-grab.com/): film stills archive. Techniques: study light and palette.
19. [Adobe Color](https://color.adobe.com/): palette tool with harmony rules. Techniques: extract palette from image.
20. [OKLCH explained (Evil Martians)](https://evilmartians.com/chronicles/oklch-in-css-why-quit-rgb-hsl): perceptual colour for UI. Techniques: uniform lightness ramps.
