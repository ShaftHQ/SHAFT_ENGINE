# Recon craft (app map, feature gap, complaint themes)

Method cards for understanding an **owned or publicly documented** product
surface before design or test work. Load one recon card when you need a
screen/flow inventory, a feature-parity matrix, or evidence-linked user
complaint themes. Do not vendor third-party clone pipelines.

| Card | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| [R01 app recon](app-recon.md) | mapping screens/flows, feature gaps, or review themes for a product you own or may improve |

Needles: `app-recon`, `features.csv`, `complaint themes`, `clean-room`.
