# L05 Cross-surface takeaways

## Use when

A technique from short-form video should carry to other surfaces. Use it
with [C11 social video](../craft/social-video.md),
[C12 training decks](../craft/training-decks.md) and
[C01 web](../craft/web-design.md).

## Rules

| Technique | Shorts | Long video | Decks and web |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Selective keyword colour: at most 1 brand-colour key word per line or cue | caption key word | lower thirds, callouts | one accent word per title |
| Pause tightening, no mid-sentence jump cuts ([L03](talk-edit-and-layers.md)) | splices at pauses | the cut list intersected with sentence ends | n/a |
| Slow push-in on static talk: 1.00 to at most 1.05, linear, face centred | static spans | any static talking head over 5 s | Ken Burns on hero images only |
| Explicit CTA end card: say the action in words, plus one button | end card | end screen | closing slide or section with 1 CTA |
| Screenshot plus a big cursor instead of a messy screen recording | n/a | a clean screenshot with an oversized cursor or a zoom to the click | screenshot with a cursor callout |
| Masked speaker over the screen (cut-out or circle PiP) | n/a | long tutorials | n/a |
| Pattern interrupt at each section change | n/a | chapter card or whoosh | full-bleed section divider |
| Button-style CTA: pill, high contrast, verb first | end card | end screen, overlay | CTA slide, primary button |

Respect reduced motion on the web: there, a push-in or interrupt becomes
a static change.

## Verify

- [ ] No line or cue has more than 1 coloured key word.
- [ ] The push-in scale stays at or under 1.05, and the face stays inside
      the centre third.
- [ ] Each surface ends with exactly 1 CTA that names a verb.
