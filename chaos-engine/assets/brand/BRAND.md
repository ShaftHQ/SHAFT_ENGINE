# ChaosEngine identity

The mark combines four controlled sweeps into an open **C** with a recessed
**E**, held around an amber core. It represents disciplined chaos, persistent
consciousness, precise support, and mechanical resolve.

These are the canonical vector masters for the portable ChaosEngine
distribution. Keep the paths synchronized; variants change application colors
or small-size treatment, not the underlying identity.

favicon for its target surface.

## Rules

- Preserve the four-sweep geometry and the recessed C/E aperture.
- Preserve clear space of at least one amber-core diameter around a standalone
  symbol or between the symbol and adjacent content.
- Use the light and dark masters as supplied; do not recolor one to imitate the
  other.
- Do not add gradients, glow, shadows, texture, pipes, rivets, facial features,
  or other illustrative detail to the canonical mark.
- Do not redraw the outlined wordmark with a live font.
- Do not rotate, skew, crop, or rearrange the sweeps.

The aesthetic is restrained armored machinery: authority and endurance without
literal character, helmet, mask, grille, or costume geometry.
