# ChaosEngine identity — Quantum Mandate

Quantum Mandate is an engineered maker's seal for disciplined
superintelligence. Four optical armor strokes orbit an offset data spine and a
cybernetic-red core. From a distance the counterform reads as an
open **C**; three logic gates reveal **E** only after attention. The tension
between order and one controlled phase shift represents ChaosEngine's
evidence-led control of uncertain work.

The method follows contemporary Chinese seal design rather than applying
Chinese decoration: compact mass, stroke economy, layered meaning, and active
positive/negative space. Its science-fiction authority stays abstract. It uses
weight, symmetry, a severe architectural brow, and abstract dark-authority
presence without copying
a helmet, mask, grille, costume, character, or franchise insignia.

## Construction and use

- Preserve the angular C counterform, three staggered gates, offset data
  spine, controlled phase break, and off-axis core. Do not center or rotate them.
- Keep clear space equal to the red-core width on every side.
- Use the supplied light, dark, monochrome, and micro masters; never simulate
  one by recoloring another.
- The canonical mark uses one neutral plus Cybernetic Red. Blue and cyan are
  for interface and data-visualization use only; never add them to the mark.
- At 16 px use only the dedicated micro-mark. It enlarges the core, merges the
  gates, and removes the smallest phase detail.
- Keep the canonical mark flat. No gradients, texture, glow, shadows, chrome,
  animation, pipes, rivets, literal gears, faces, helmets, or franchise cues.
- Do not redraw the outlined wordmark with a live font.

The visual voice is ancient authority rebuilt as advanced cybernetic
machinery: restrained, recognizable, culturally informed, and original.
