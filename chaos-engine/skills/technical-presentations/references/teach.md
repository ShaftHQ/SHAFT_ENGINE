# Teaching rhythm

## 10 Retrieval checks

- Recall slide at the start of each chapter: 2-3 questions on the last one.
- Checkpoint every 7-10 slides: one concept question, 3-4 options. Vote
  alone, discuss, vote again (peer instruction). Answer and explanation on
  the next slide or in the notes. Low stakes, feedback always.
- Recap slide per section with a repeated visual stamp.

## 11 Worked examples

- Novices: a fully worked example first, then a faded one ("you finish it"),
  then a solo task. Fade steps one at a time; add self-explanation prompts.
- Experts: skip straight to the faded or open problem.

## 12 Voice and demos

- Humour tied to the concept, takeaway restated right after the joke, never
  at a learner's expense.
- Conversational tone beats formal (personalization).
- Live demo: fonts large before the session, zoom ready, line numbers on,
  toolbars hidden, one-click reset, a backup recording, deterministic targets.
