## Audio (#6704)

The background noise was the synthetic ambient bed: a 110 Hz-based sine chord with echo, about -42 dBFS between sentences after loudness normalization. The Kokoro narration itself measures digital silence in gaps and -73 to -83 dBFS in pauses inside lines. v2 removes the bed. It fills each scene change longer than 0.7 s with one quiet transition swell (G4-D5-G5, high-passed, no echo). The narration stem is high-passed at 70 Hz and set to -16 LUFS. The master is set to -14 LUFS with linear gain and a peak limiter.
